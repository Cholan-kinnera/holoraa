## 🎯 Mission
**Give people one simple, safe place to talk, share and hang out with the people who matter to them.**

## 🔭 Vision
**A world where staying close to your people never needs five different apps.**

## 💎 Core Values

1. **Simple by default:** Clean, focused interfaces without clutter or bloated toolbars.
2. **Safe by design:** Built-in protections against harassment, abuse, spam, and non-consensual interactions.
3. **Private by default:** User data is treated as private, encrypted in transit, and tightly restricted.
4. **Respectful of attention:** No attention-trapping notifications and no artificial badges designed to create compulsive checking.
5. **Built in the open where possible:** Transparent architecture, public roadmap, and verifiable security practices.

---

## 🚫 Non-Goals

- **No advertising:** No ad networks, tracking pixels, or data brokering.
- **No selling user data:** Profiles and activity are never sold or shared with third parties.
- **No algorithmic feed manipulation:** Any feed stays chronological and user-driven, with no ranking designed to maximise time spent.
- **No attention extraction:** No streaks, no anxiety-inducing unread counts, and no feature whose main purpose is inflating screen time.

---

## 👥 Target Users

1. **Close friend groups:** People who find WhatsApp too rigid for hangouts and Discord too chaotic.
2. **Small focused communities:** Study groups, research circles, independent creators, and alumni networks that need organised channels without enterprise overhead.
3. **Digital minimalists:** People tired of notification overload and of spreading conversations across several apps.

---

## 🎨 Design Principles

- **Minimalist:** High signal-to-noise ratio.
- **Dark mode first:** Deep navy backgrounds for comfortable reading over long periods. See [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).
- **Rounded shapes:** Friendly, modern corners.
- **One gradient accent:** A single signature gradient, used deliberately for primary actions.
- **Secondary controls behind "+":** Advanced actions sit in a sheet, keeping the main view calm.
